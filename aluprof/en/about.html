About us

Who we are

A manufacturer responsible for every metre

ALUPROF is an Uzbek manufacturer of aluminium profile systems for windows, doors, shopfronts and façades.

The company has been operating since 2010. Today we have two production sites in the Tashkent Region and a team of more than 100 people.

We keep the key stages in-house, from extrusion to warehousing. This makes it easier to take responsibility for profile quality and delivery times.

Our products are grouped into three lines: THERMO thermal systems with the Silver 64 and Platinum 78 series, the FASAD façade line and the non-insulated ALDOX line.

Our partners are window and door manufacturers, dealers, architects and developers in Uzbekistan and Kyrgyzstan.

Principles

  • Inspection before shipping

  • Agreed timelines
